About Calibration
Why Calibrate?

Calibration compensates for variations in tank geometry, sensor placement, and fluid properties that might affect the accuracy of volume calculations.

How It Works

The system calculates a calibration factor by comparing the measured volume (based on pressure) with the known actual volume. This factor is then applied to all future measurements.

When to Calibrate
  • After initial installation
  • When changing fluid types
  • If measurements seem inaccurate
  • Periodically for maintenance
Best Practices
  • Use a reliable method to determine the actual volume
  • Perform calibration when the tank is at least 25% full
  • Ensure the fluid is settled and not in motion
  • Verify results after calibration
